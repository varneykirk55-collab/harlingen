# Geographic accuracy and attribution

| Layer | Measured or mapped input | Modeled interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terrain | 2401 × 2401 USGS 3DEP bare-earth pixels at 5 m requested sampling interval. Source grid spans about −0.008 to 24.504 m | Resampled geometry and Terrain3D R16 quantization. Water surfaces, terrain borders and road-surface offsets are modeled |
| Municipal extent | U.S. Census TIGERweb incorporated place, GEOID 4832372 | 86.54 km² inside the 144 km² build, out of 109.06 km² in the source place polygon. Some annexed land lies beyond the square |
| Ground color | USDA NAIP coverage delivered by the USGS National Map and received on November 20, 2026 | Aerial color blended into the terrain. Individual imagery acquisition dates were unavailable in the received export |
| Roads and paths | OSM centerlines and linked tags for class, lanes, width, surface, bridges and sidewalks where present | Widths inferred when untagged. Road paint, curb placement and bridge elevation are approximations. Some ramps and overpasses need survey or lidar checks |
| Buildings | Clipped OSM and Overture footprints at projected coordinates | 32,642 modeled polygon shells. 18,406 use source height values, often derived from remote sensing. 298 use source floor counts, 1 uses a documented landmark floor count, and 13,937 use inferred heights. Common residential rooflines and exterior materials are procedural. Some footprints can be missing, outdated or misclassified |
| Landmarks | Mapped positions, outlines and access networks. Baxter Building National Register documentation | Baxter's nine-story tower is individually modeled. The airport uses mapped runway, taxiway and terminal footprints. Lon C. Hill Park, McKelvey Park and Hugh Ramsey Nature Park use mapped land usage and paths. Other landmark buildings are generally shells |
| Streetscape | Mapped signals, utility poles, crossings, some lamps, benches and trees | Signal poles are shifted clear of mapped pavement where the underlying location indicates an intersection center. Added lighting, palms, mesquite and brush use illustrative placements. Signal assemblies and signs are generic |
| Hydrography | OSM centerlines/polygons for Arroyo Colorado, canals, drains, ditches and lakes | Untagged widths and water levels are approximate. Water is a static asset layer |
| Architecture | Harlingen's downtown plan and public historical records | Procedural façade atlases and generic commercial, residential and industrial exterior treatments. Detailed storefronts, airport interiors, residential addresses, utility wires, surveyed curb/sidewalk profiles and photogrammetry remain outside this pass |
