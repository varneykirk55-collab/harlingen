# Downtown streetscape

Harlingen uses 5,987 instanced palms, including 4,779 replacements at the previous tree positions. Each palm has 163 triangles. Smaller scrub shrubs use rounded crowns, and 13 dry-grass clumps mark open ground and lot edges west of South A Street and around the downtown blocks.

The downtown district has an editable sign scene at `assets/signage/DowntownStreetscape.tscn`. The seven bilingual façade signs use flat textured panels at walking height. Business-type text is illustrative and was placed on mapped frontages. The four street-name posts at Jackson Avenue use English street names from the project data and Spanish translations on both sides.

From the first-person start, turn toward West Jackson Avenue to see the taquería and wine shop signs. Walk east along Jackson Avenue toward 1st, 2nd and 3rd Streets for the other street-name posts. The market sign is beside the Baxter Building.

The previous release's district geometry, footprints, road and terrain data, and collision meshes remain byte-identical. The sign props are attached to the existing downtown district scene.
